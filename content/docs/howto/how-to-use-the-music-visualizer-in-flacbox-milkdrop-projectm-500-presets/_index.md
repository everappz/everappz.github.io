## What Is the Flacbox Visualizer?

The visualizer is a moving light show that reacts to your music in real time. As the song plays, the sound drives the shapes, colors, and motion on screen, so quiet moments look calm and loud moments burst with energy. It is great on a big screen, on a Mac, or just to enjoy the music in a new way.

Under the hood it uses **Milkdrop-style presets** through the open-source **projectM** engine, the same family of visuals many people remember from desktop music players. Each preset is a different animated scene, and Flacbox ships with **500 of them**. The visuals are drawn with **OpenGL** for smooth, GPU-accelerated animation.

## How to Turn On the Visualizer

There are two ways to open it.

**From the player (fastest):**

1. Open the **Now Playing** player screen.
2. Tap the **⋯ (More)** button.
3. Tap **Visualization**.

**From Settings:**

1. Go to the **Settings** tab.
2. Tap **Visualization**.

Either way, you land on the **preset picker**.

## The Preset Picker

The preset picker is a simple list where you choose how the visualizer starts:

- **Auto** sits at the top of the list. Pick it to let Flacbox shuffle through the presets on its own, changing to a new one every **30 seconds** with a smooth **crossfade**. This is the easiest way to enjoy the show without touching anything.
- **Any named preset** (the list is sorted by name) starts the visualizer on that exact look. The preset you used last is remembered and highlighted, so it is easy to find again.

Once you have made your choice, tap **Start visualization** and the full-screen show begins. As the app puts it: *"Select a preset to start with it, or use Auto mode which shuffles through the presets, switching every 30 seconds with a smooth crossfade."*

## The Controls on the Visualizer Screen

The visualizer runs full-screen so nothing gets in the way of the visuals. **Tap anywhere once** to bring up the controls, and tap again (or just wait a few seconds) to hide them so you get a clean, full-screen picture.

When the controls are showing, you will see:

- **Preset title and counter (top center).** The name of the current preset, with a counter underneath, like **429 / 500**, so you always know which one you are looking at and how many there are. In the screenshot above, the current preset is **Starfield Sectors**.
- **Previous and Next arrows (bottom).** Tap **Next (>)** to jump to the next look, or **Previous (<)** to go back. This lets you flip through the presets by hand until you find one you love.
- **Close button (top corner).** Tap it to leave the visualizer and return to the app.

The screen also **stays awake** while the visualizer is on, so it will not dim or lock in the middle of the show.

## How to Change the Preset

You have two easy ways to change the look:

1. **By hand.** Tap the screen to show the controls, then use the **Previous** and **Next** arrows to move through the presets one at a time. The title and counter at the top update as you go, and Flacbox remembers the last preset you landed on for next time.
2. **Automatically.** Start the visualizer in **Auto** mode (from the preset picker). Flacbox then shuffles through the presets for you, switching every **30 seconds** with a gentle crossfade, so the show keeps changing on its own.

## iPhone, iPad, and Mac

The visualizer works across Flacbox on **iOS and macOS**:

- **On iPhone and iPad**, it fills the screen and is drawn with **OpenGL ES**, so it stays smooth even on a Retina display.
- **On Mac**, Flacbox opens the visualizer in its own window and draws it with native **desktop OpenGL**, so you get the same reactive Milkdrop visuals on the big screen.

Either way, the visuals react to the exact audio you are playing, whether that is a local FLAC file, a track from a cloud drive or media server, or an internet radio stream.

## Tips

- **Tap once to hide the controls** for a clean, full-screen picture, then tap again to bring them back.
- **Try Auto mode** the first time, then switch to picking presets by hand once you find styles you like.
- **The counter (like 429 / 500)** helps you remember favorites, so you can flip back to a preset you enjoyed.
- **Keep it playing.** The visuals follow the music, so the more dynamic the track, the more the scene moves.
- **On Mac**, resize the visualizer window to fit your display or a second screen.
