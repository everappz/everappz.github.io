{{% sr-only %}}
Everdisk is a wireless drive app for iPhone and iPad developed by Everappz. It turns your device into a hub that shares files, photos, videos and music with anything on your local network, with no accounts, no cloud and no extra app to install on the other side. The app runs four servers at the same time - DLNA for smart TVs and media players, HTTP for any web browser, WebDAV for Finder, Windows and Linux, and FTP for file apps and power users - so every device connects the way it likes. You can stream photos, videos and music straight to smart TVs over DLNA with preview thumbnails, share a simple link that opens in any browser to browse and download your files, or mount your device as a network drive and drag files in both directions. When there is no Wi-Fi you can connect to a Mac with a USB cable and move files faster than Wi-Fi, even on a plane or a locked network. Everdisk also connects to DLNA, WebDAV, FTP and SFTP servers on your network to browse, stream and download their files. A built-in file manager lets you browse in list or grid, create folders, rename, move, copy, zip and unzip, and delete. You can scan paper documents to clean PDFs, open a PDF or photo and mark it up with the built-in editor - draw, highlight, add text and a signature, with changes saved back to the file - and use a full set of PDF tools to compress files, recognize text (OCR) into a searchable PDF, add or remove an open password, review permissions, edit document metadata, and flatten annotations and form fields. You can also play audio in a mini player and watch videos full screen, and share your whole photo and music library over the network in original quality or a more compatible format. Everything stays on your local network and never touches the internet, and you can protect access with a login and password and block any device with one tap. Everdisk is a free download on the App Store with an optional one-time Premium Lifetime purchase.

{{% details title="What is Everdisk?" closed="true" %}}
Everdisk turns your iPhone or iPad into a wireless drive and a hub that connects to your other devices too.<br>
You can share your files, photos, videos and music with anything on your network, browse and stream from other servers, and manage everything right on your device.<br>
There are no accounts, no cloud, and no extra app to install on the other side - you just tap Start and you are ready.<br><br>

The app runs four servers at the same time, so every device connects the way it likes: smart TVs over DLNA, computers and web browsers over HTTP, Finder, Windows and Linux over WebDAV, and file apps over FTP.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="How does Everdisk work?" closed="true" %}}
When you tap Start, Everdisk begins sharing the content you selected and runs four servers at once so different devices can connect in the way they prefer.<br><br>

- <strong>DLNA</strong> lets smart TVs and media players find your photos, videos and music automatically and show them with preview thumbnails.<br>
- <strong>HTTP</strong> gives anyone a simple link they can open in a web browser to browse, preview and download your files, with nothing to install.<br>
- <strong>WebDAV</strong> makes your device appear as a normal network drive in Finder, Windows or Linux so you can drag files in both directions.<br>
- <strong>FTP</strong> works with file apps and power users.<br><br>

Everdisk also connects the other way, to DLNA, WebDAV, FTP and SFTP servers on your network, so you can browse their folders, stream audio and video, and download or manage files when the server allows it. Everything stays on your local network and never touches the internet.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="Is Everdisk free?" closed="true" %}}
Yes. Everdisk is a free download and you can turn your device into a wireless drive, share your files four ways, connect to other servers, and use the built-in file manager at no cost.<br><br>

There is an optional one-time <strong>Premium Lifetime</strong> purchase - a single payment with no subscription - that unlocks unlimited shared folders and saved connections, photo &amp; video conversion, custom ports, auto-start sharing, and device customization. It is tied to your Apple ID, so you can use Restore Purchases to unlock it on your other devices. Prices may vary depending on your country or region.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="How do I connect from my computer?" closed="true" %}}
Everdisk makes your device appear as a normal network drive over WebDAV.<br><br>

- On a <strong>Mac</strong>, open Finder, choose <em>Go → Connect to Server</em>, and enter the WebDAV address shown in the app.<br>
- On <strong>Windows</strong>, map a network drive using the same WebDAV address.<br>
- On <strong>Linux</strong>, connect to the WebDAV address from your file manager.<br><br>

Once connected, you can drag and drop in both directions: copy files from your computer to your device, or pull them back. You can also open the HTTP link in any web browser if you prefer not to mount a drive.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="How do I stream to my TV?" closed="true" %}}
Make sure your TV or media player and your device are on the same Wi-Fi network, then tap Start in Everdisk with your photos, videos or music shared.<br><br>

Your device will appear by itself in the list of media servers on the TV, complete with preview thumbnails. Open it on the TV and enjoy your library on the big screen - no cables and no extra apps required.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="How do I open my files in a web browser?" closed="true" %}}
Tap Start and Everdisk gives you a simple web address for the HTTP server.<br><br>

Share that link with anyone on the same network. They open it in any web browser to browse your files in a clean grid or list, view photos in a full-screen gallery, play music in the built-in player, and download anything. There is nothing to install on their end, which makes it the quickest way to hand files to another phone or computer.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="Does Everdisk work without Wi-Fi?" closed="true" %}}
Yes. Connect your device to a Mac with the same USB cable you use to charge it, and files move straight through the cable, faster than Wi-Fi.<br><br>

Because it does not depend on a wireless network, this keeps working on a plane, in a hotel, or on any locked or public network where Wi-Fi sharing is blocked.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="What can I connect to with Everdisk?" closed="true" %}}
Everdisk is also a client for other devices on your network. You can find and connect to <strong>DLNA</strong>, <strong>WebDAV</strong>, <strong>FTP</strong> and <strong>SFTP</strong> servers, including NAS devices and media servers.<br><br>

Once connected, you can browse their folders, stream audio and video, download files, and create folders, upload, rename, move or delete when the server allows it.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="Can I scan documents and work with PDFs?" closed="true" %}}
Yes. Everdisk can scan paper documents with your camera - it finds the edges automatically, straightens every page, and saves them as a clean multi-page PDF right on your device.<br><br>

You can also open a PDF (or photo) and <strong>mark it up</strong> right in the built-in viewer - draw, highlight, add text and shapes, and add a signature with your finger - and your changes are saved back to the file.<br><br>

On top of that, Everdisk includes a full set of <strong>PDF Tools</strong>, opened from a PDF's <strong>•••</strong> menu in the Files browser:<br>
• <strong>Compress</strong> - shrink a PDF, with an estimated size shown before you save.<br>
• <strong>Text Recognition (OCR)</strong> - turn a scan into a searchable PDF, or export the text to a .txt file.<br>
• <strong>Password</strong> - set, change or remove the password needed to open the PDF.<br>
• <strong>Permissions</strong> - review what the PDF allows (printing, copying, and more).<br>
• <strong>Edit Metadata</strong> - change or clear the title, author, keywords and other document info.<br>
• <strong>Flatten</strong> - burn annotations and form entries into the page so they can't be changed.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="Is Everdisk private and safe?" closed="true" %}}
Everything stays on your local network and never touches the internet, so your files never leave home. There are no accounts and no cloud in the middle.<br><br>

You can protect access with a login and password so connected devices must enter the same details before they can see your files, and you can block any device on your network with a single tap. For the best privacy, turn sharing on only while you are connected to a Wi-Fi network you know and trust.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="What can I do with the file manager?" closed="true" %}}
Everdisk includes a full file manager for everything on your device.<br><br>

You can browse in list or grid, create folders, rename, move, copy, zip and unzip, and delete. You can also connect folders from anywhere on your device and work with them right in the app, then share them over the network alongside everything else.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="Can I play music inside the app?" closed="true" %}}
Yes. Tap any audio file and it plays in the mini player that stays at the bottom of the screen while you keep browsing.<br><br>

The mini player has a full queue with shuffle and repeat (off, all, or one), and lock screen and media-key controls that show the real title, artist and album. Tap or swipe up to open the full player, and swipe down to shrink it while the audio keeps playing. Tapping one track in a folder plays the whole folder as a queue.<br><br>

Audio plays from your local files, connected folders, your Music library, and servers you connect to on the Devices tab.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="Can I watch videos in the app?" closed="true" %}}
Yes. Tap a video to play it full screen. Everdisk uses the built-in system video player, so you get the familiar controls, including AirPlay, Picture in Picture, and playback speed.<br><br>

Videos play from your local files and connected folders, or stream straight from a device you connect to on the Devices tab, so you do not have to download the whole file first.
{{% /details %}}

{{% details title="How do I view my photos?" closed="true" %}}
Open the Documents tab and switch to Photos mode to browse your photos and videos. Tap a photo to open it full screen, where you can zoom, swipe between photos, and share.<br><br>
